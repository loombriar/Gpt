Pawdrey Hepburn — Spirit of the Briar Woods

Meet Pawdrey Hepburn

Pawdrey Hepburn is the gray dilute tortie guardian of Loom Briar — recognizable by her clipped left ear and the upside-down moon marking on her forehead.

She is more than a mascot. Pawdrey Hepburn is the quiet spirit who leads visitors through the Briar Woods and anchors the Loom Briar emblem.

A handmade world

Art meant to feel discovered, not manufactured.

Loom Briar is an enchanted woodland artist lifestyle brand built around original art, clothing, art prints, home goods, and signature hand-painted thermochromic keepsake boxes.

The visual language is storybook rather than storefront: moss, moonlight, lantern glow, fireflies, crystals, quiet paths, and the feeling of entering a world hidden behind the briars.
